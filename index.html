About

Abhijeet Goraksha Bharati is a SET-qualified academic and corporate professional with a blend of technical expertise and managerial experience. With a Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering and a Master's degree in Management Studies (Systems), he brings a cross-disciplinary perspective that combines problem-solving, systems thinking, and business management.

His career spans sales, branch management, regional risk and audit, and corporate training. In corporate roles he designed training programmes to improve operational efficiency and employee development, and he developed the Employee Point System, a performance measurement and recognition system later implemented across India.

As a faculty member, he follows a student-centric teaching philosophy. He emphasises applying concepts to real business problems and helps students develop adaptability, analytical thinking, and leadership skills for today's technology-driven business environment.
